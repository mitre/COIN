# Inquiry

Inquiry is a proposed interaction model based on an iterative exchange of
questions and supported answers. Either participant can ask questions, seek
clarification, or request supporting evidence. The conversation guides what
information to retrieve and what to ask next.

It could support tasks in which the information needed emerges during the
conversation, including tasks without an evaluation or a predefined set of
requirements.

In [Disclosure](../disclosure/index.md), the Evaluator supplies applicable
[Criteria](../criteria/index.md) upfront. Inquiry would proceed through questions
and answers without using Criteria.

## Roles

The Preparer gathers information and prepares supported answers and evidence.
The Evaluator asks and refines questions, reviews the information supplied, and
identifies what further information is needed. These roles describe the work
performed across the interaction, and either participant can ask questions.

## Information and review

The Preparer would search authorized sources and return answers with supporting
references. Answers could be partial or report that the requested information
could not be found, with gaps and uncertainty made clear. Either participant
could ask for clarification, request supporting evidence, or follow up on the
information exchanged.

Inquiry could also support evaluation workflows. A [Profile](../profile/index.md)
would define how the exchange fits within the broader workflow, including the
participants, review, and use of the information gathered.
